War of Rights: War of 1812 (1812-15)
====================================

Version 1.3.5
Built for War of Rights Steam build 26270438 (October 28, 2026).

The United States take the USA slot; Britain and its Canadian, colonial and
First Nations allies take the CSA slot.


WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.5
-------------------
- American regulars now wear caps, not round hats. Nine U.S. Infantry
  regiments (the 4th, 7th, 13th, 14th, 17th, 19th, 27th, 29th and 44th) had
  nothing but round hats in their kit. Their Hardee hats and high-crowned
  slouch hats are now the 1811 felt cap, so the 4th, 7th, 17th and 19th are
  all in caps and the rest mostly. Low-crowned slouch hats and campaign hats
  are still round hats, the militia's share, and the Kentucky Rifles,
  Coffee's and Pinkney's keep their own round hats.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.4
-------------------
- Updated for the War of Rights patch of October 28, 2026 (Steam build
  26270438). The mod's weapon data, first-person animations and scoreboard
  now include the patch's own changes, so the mod works with the new game
  version.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.3
-------------------
- The dapple-grey horse is a grey horse again. It had been turned into a
  pack mule, a leftover from the Mexican-American War mod this one grew out
  of.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.2
-------------------
- Artillery aiming: the guns no longer block your view when laying them.
  Each barrel sits on its trunnions the way the base game's guns do and is
  set at the angle that keeps it under your line of sight.
- The 12-pdr Napoleon 1857 now carries the British 9-pounder (Britain's main
  heavy field gun of 1813-15).


INSTALL
-------
Easiest: install it with the WoR Mod Manager, which also keeps it updated.

By hand:
1. Open your War of Rights folder. In Steam: right-click War of Rights >
   Manage > Browse local files.
2. Open the Assets folder.
3. Copy every z_w12_*.pak file from this zip into Assets. Put them straight
   into Assets, not into a subfolder.
4. Start the game.


UPDATE
------
Delete all the old z_w12_*.pak files from Assets first, then copy in the new
ones. Some files may be added or dropped between versions, so don't just
copy over the top.


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete every z_w12_*.pak file from Assets. Nothing else is changed.

Only have one period mod installed at a time: the Mexican-American War,
War of 1812 and Crimean War mods replace the same game files.


AFTER A GAME UPDATE
-------------------
When War of Rights gets a patch, remove the mod until an updated version is
released (the WoR Mod Manager picks it up for you).


PLAYING ONLINE
--------------
The mod only changes what you see. You can join normal servers, and other
players don't need it installed. They will see the vanilla game, and you
will see everyone in 1812-15 uniforms.

The muskets and rifles keep one fixed sight setting, as the arms of 1812-15
had: the mouse wheel no longer steps the rear sight while aiming.
