War of 1812 1.3.0

- Light dragoons' overalls no longer clip through the riding boots. They are now worn over the boot, as overalls were, still grey with the double yellow stripe.
- American infantry gaiters (grey and white trousers): no longer skinny or pinched at the knee. Each gaiter is now one piece of black wool from the shoe to just below the knee, without the old ridge at mid-calf or the knitted-stocking look. The trousers no longer show through at the calf and heel, and the buttons sit on the cloth.
- The 3rd Ohio's leggings get the same fixes.

Client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.
