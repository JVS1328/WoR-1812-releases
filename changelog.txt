War of 1812 1.3.1

- Updated for the War of Rights patch of October 26, 2026 (Steam build 26223473). The mod's weapon data and player animations now include the patch's own changes, so the mod works with the new game version.
- Flintlock pistols: between shots they cock with the base game's revolver motion again, the cock and pan moving with it. The two-handed flintlock handling ran longer than the game allows and was cut off mid-way.
- Scoped Whitworth and Sharps rifle items: the leftover scope lens no longer shows in first person (a clear disc and a black disc near the sights). They aim with the plain rifle's iron sight.

Client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.
