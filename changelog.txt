War of 1812 1.4.1

- The last mixed-up kit is gone. 1.4.0 dressed almost every regiment in its own uniform; these units still had a few odd coats or trousers and now wear only their own:
  - the Naval Brigade, all in seamen's jackets;
  - the American artillery's majors and colonels, in artillery officers' coats;
  - the 104th Foot, all in buff-faced coats;
  - the 93rd Highlanders, officers included, all in tartan trews, with shoes and gaiters or boots.
- NCOs and officers in these coats wear the rank insignia made for them.
