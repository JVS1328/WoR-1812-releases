War of 1812 1.2.0

- Winter greatcoats: the Americans' greatcoats are no longer the Civil War sky blue. They are now the 1810 regulation watch coat cloth, a dark drab woven with scarlet, with white-metal buttons.
- US officers' long coats (1812 and 1813): the cuffs and skirts are now the same blue as the coat instead of a lighter shade. The regiment portraits are updated to match.

Client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.
