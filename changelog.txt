War of 1812 1.3.5

- American regulars now wear caps, not round hats. Nine U.S. Infantry regiments (the 4th, 7th, 13th, 14th, 17th, 19th, 27th, 29th and 44th) had nothing but round hats in their kit. Their Hardee hats and high-crowned slouch hats are now the 1811 felt cap, so the 4th, 7th, 17th and 19th are all in caps and the rest mostly. Low-crowned slouch hats and campaign hats are still round hats, the militia's share, and the Kentucky Rifles, Coffee's and Pinkney's keep their own round hats.
