War of 1812 1.3.2

- Artillery aiming: the guns no longer block your view when laying them. Each barrel now sits on its trunnions the way the base game's guns do and is set at the angle that keeps it under your line of sight, so the aiming point stays visible over the barrel, as on the vanilla pieces.
- The 12-pdr Napoleon 1857 now carries the British 9-pounder (Britain's main heavy field gun of 1813-15). The medium 12-pounder it carried before was too big at the breech and too long at the muzzle to stay out of the gunner's view at any angle.

Client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.
